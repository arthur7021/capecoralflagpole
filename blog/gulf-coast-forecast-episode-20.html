The Gulf Coast Forecast

Published August 3, 2026

Burrowing Al steps up to the forecast board by the flagpole for a straight weather report, and Cape Coral immediately proves him wrong. One family packs for four different seasons before lunch. Al's own forecast app and the actual sky stop speaking to each other mid-segment. The flag whips through a sudden downpour, and by sunset it's back to a rainbow and an iced coffee like nothing happened. Al's read on it: the app isn't broken, Florida just doesn't believe in forecasts.

Why one afternoon feels like four seasons here

Anyone who's lived near the Gulf Coast for a summer knows the drill: bright sun at noon, a wall of rain by 2pm, and a rainbow over the bridge before dinner. It's not a forecasting failure, it's just how Southwest Florida weather works in the wet season, and it makes every actual forecast app look like it's guessing.

Burrowing Al's version just puts a face on the chaos everyone in Cape Coral already lives through on a near-daily basis, which is exactly why it lands.

Burrowing Al's official takeaway: pack for all four seasons, every single day.
